About TRUVIC Machinery

A China-based supplier of metal, tube and packaging processing machinery. Seven production lines, 30+ models, factory-direct pricing and one English-speaking contact from quote to spare parts.

Who we are

TRUVIC Machinery Co., Ltd. is an export supplier of industrial processing machinery based in China. We are a young company — and we treat that as a promise rather than a gap: every machine we quote is one we can inspect at the factory ourselves before it ships, and every specification we publish is one we can back with a drawing, a video or a test report.

Our range covers seven production lines: welding and cutting, sheet-metal and laser processing, fin tube processing, heat exchanger tube processing, transformer and busbar processing, rim making, and paper and plastic packaging machinery.

What we supply

  • Welding Machinery — spot, MIG/TIG and automatic seam welders for sheet-metal and tube work.
  • Sheet-Metal and Laser Machinery — fiber laser cutting, press brake and shearing equipment.
  • Fin Tube Processing Machinery — extruded and wound fin tube lines for heat exchangers.
  • Heat Exchanger Tube Processing Machinery — copper tube straightening, cutting and return bending.
  • Transformer & Busbar Processing Machinery — busbar punching/bending machines and step-lap CRGO core cutting lines.
  • Rim Making Machinery — flash butt welding, roll forming, spinning and hydraulic rim flaring.
  • Paper & Plastic Processing Machinery — cup, lid, bag and box forming machines.

How a machine reaches your floor

  • 01 · Requirement. You send the working spec: material, thickness or tube diameter, output per shift, voltage and any mould or product size.
  • 02 · Shortlist and quote. We reply within one business day (Mon–Fri, GMT+8) with a model recommendation, key parameters and a factory-direct price.
  • 03 · Verification. Before dispatch you receive machine photos, a running video and, on request, a third-party inspection report.
  • 04 · Packing and export. Plywood-case packing, container loading plan, commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin.
  • 05 · After shipment. Installation and commissioning guidance, operation manuals in English, and a spare-parts list issued with the order.

Why buyers work with us

  • Factory-direct. We buy from the manufacturing base and quote the factory price, not a stack of agent margins.
  • One contact. The same person answers your enquiry, follows the production schedule and handles the spare-parts order afterwards.
  • Evidence first. Parameters come from datasheets and test runs, not from a catalogue template.
  • Export paperwork done properly. Documents are checked before the container leaves, so clearance does not stall on our side.
