Why this machine pays back

Three things buyers actually ask about on a fin tube line: bonding quality, die changeover time, and whether it runs continuously without stopping for jams.

Tight fin-to-tube bonding

Extruded rather than glued or welded: the aluminium is formed onto the base tube so there is no contact resistance, and thermal performance stays stable over service life.

Die change in minutes

Fin height and pitch are set by the die set, not by shimming. A die swap takes minutes, so one machine covers several tube specifications.

Continuous mass production

Rigid frame and linear guides hold straightness on long runs, with low failure rate on the tooling — the machine is built to keep running, not to be repaired.
