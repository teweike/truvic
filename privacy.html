Privacy Policy

What data this website collects, why it is collected, who processes it, and how you can have it removed.

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • Enquiry data you submit: name, company, email, phone or WhatsApp number, country, machine interest, quantity, timeline and the message you write.
  • Technical data: browser type, device type, approximate region and pages visited, collected through standard server logs and, where enabled, analytics.

Why we collect it

Enquiry data is used only to answer your request, prepare a quotation, arrange shipping documents and follow up on an order. Technical data is used to keep the site working and to understand which product pages are useful.

Who processes it

  • Our own sales team, for quoting and order handling.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers the enquiry form to our mailbox. The form is not stored in a public database.
  • Our email provider and, where analytics is enabled, a web analytics service.
  • Shipping lines, forwarders and inspection companies — only the data needed to move your order.

We do not sell, rent or trade contact data.

Cookies

The site itself does not require cookies to display product information. Third-party services such as analytics or embedded fonts may set cookies; you can block them in your browser without losing access to product pages.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and order records are kept as long as needed to support the commercial relationship and to meet accounting and export-record obligations. Marketing follow-up stops as soon as you ask.

Your choices

Write to teweike@foxmail.com to request a copy of the data we hold, correct it, or have it deleted. Requests are handled within 30 days.
