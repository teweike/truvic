Frequently Asked Questions

The questions buyers ask most before a first order: minimum quantity, lead time, payment terms, shipping, warranty and spare parts.

What is the minimum order quantity?

One machine. We do not require a container or a multi-unit order before quoting. For small machines we can also quote a shared container or LCL shipment.

How long is the lead time?

Standard models are typically 15–30 days after deposit. Custom moulds, special voltage or a full production line usually add 10–20 days. The exact date is written into the proforma invoice, not just promised in email.

What payment terms do you accept?

Standard terms are 30% T/T deposit with 70% before shipment, or an irrevocable L/C at sight for larger orders. The bank details on the proforma invoice are the only valid payment route — always confirm by phone if anything looks different.

Which shipping terms can you quote?

EXW, FOB Shanghai or Ningbo, and CIF to your port. We can also quote door-to-door by sea or rail where the route allows. Machines travel in plywood cases with fumigation-free packing.

What warranty comes with the machine?

12 months from commissioning or 13 months from shipment, whichever comes first, covering core mechanical and electrical components. Wear parts, consumables and damage from incorrect operation are excluded — the spare-parts list in the manual shows what to stock.

How do spare parts work after delivery?

A recommended spare-parts list is issued with the order. Common wear parts are kept in stock and can ship by courier within a few days; larger components are quoted with lead time. Send the machine serial number and a photo or video of the part.

Can the machine be built for our voltage and our product size?

Yes. Voltage and frequency (380V 50Hz, 415V 50Hz, 480V 60Hz and others), plug type, mould dimensions and product size are confirmed in writing before production starts.

Can we inspect before shipment?

Yes. You are welcome to visit the factory, or we can send a running video and a photo set of your actual machine before it is packed. A third-party inspection such as SGS or BV can be arranged at your cost.

Do you provide installation support?

Every machine ships with an English operation manual and wiring diagram. Remote video support is included. On-site commissioning can be arranged for production lines and larger equipment; travel and accommodation are quoted separately.

Still unanswered? Send us the question — we reply within one business day.
