Electron Beam Welding Machine

A high-vacuum electron beam welding system built around a 6 kW triode gun. A focused beam delivers up to 20 mm penetration in stainless steel in a single pass, at a depth-to-width ratio above 13:1, with the narrow heat-affected zone and zero oxidation that only a vacuum process gives you. Both vertical and horizontal circular seam welding are supported, driven by a 300 × 300 × 300 mm chamber with independent horizontal and vertical rotary tables.

Key Advantages

Six things that matter when your part has a deep, narrow, vacuum-tight joint.

20 mm in one pass

A 6 kW beam at up to 60 kV welds 20 mm of stainless steel without pre-machining a V-groove or running multiple passes — one of the few processes that does this in a single shot.

13:1 depth-to-width

The narrow seam means minimal distortion and a heat-affected zone measured in tenths of a millimetre, so post-weld machining and straightening drop away.

Vacuum = no oxidation

Welding below 5 × 10⁻² Pa removes oxygen from the process entirely. Reactive metals, titanium and copper joints come out clean with no shielding gas and no post-weld pickling.

Both seam directions

Horizontal rotary table (0–30 rpm) with pneumatic tailstock for shafts and rings; vertical table (0–50 rpm) with a three-jaw chuck for discs. Run-out held within ±0.03 mm on both.

Gun stays under vacuum

The gun chamber is pumped independently and isolated by a valve, so it holds vacuum while you vent and reload the work chamber — reloads cost only the 150-second chamber pump-down, not a full gun evacuation.

Recipe-driven control

OMRON PLC with touch-screen HMI: stored welding programs, beam up/down slope control, scan frequency and amplitude, live readout of every parameter, plus interlocks and water/gas alarms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on the points that usually decide the order.

What wall thickness can this machine weld in one pass?

20 mm in stainless steel at full 6 kW gun power. Depth-to-width ratio exceeds 13:1, so a 20 mm seam is roughly 1.5 mm wide at the root. Carbon steel and copper reach comparable depths at lower power; send us the material and section drawing and we confirm the exact pass count.

Do I need to prepare a groove or edge preparation before welding?

No V-groove is required for most joints. Square butt faces with a clean machined finish are enough — the beam penetrates and produces the seam itself. That removes a machining step and the associated fixturing.

How long does a cycle take including vacuum?

The chamber pumps down to better than 5 × 10⁻² Pa in 150 seconds or less. The gun chamber is pumped independently and stays under vacuum across reloads, so you only pay the chamber time each cycle, not a full gun evacuation.

Can it weld circular seams on both shafts and discs?

Yes. A horizontal rotary table with a pneumatic tailstock handles shafts and rings at 0–30 rpm; a vertical rotary table with a three-jaw chuck handles discs at 0–50 rpm. Both run at ±0.03 mm run-out so seam position stays repeatable.

What about radiation safety and certification?

Chamber walls are ≥ 38 mm with X-ray leakage ≤ 0.25 mRem. The machine ships with an X-ray safety certificate signed by the authority, plus interlocked doors and automatic protection on fault conditions. Our team walks your EHS contact through the local registration requirements.
