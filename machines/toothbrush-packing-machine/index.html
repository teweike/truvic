Toothbrush Packing Machine

A vertical form-fill-seal machine that bags toothbrushes in back-seal pillow packs at up to 90 bags per minute. Film runs from a roll through the former, the brush drops from the feeder into the tube, and the seal jaws close and cut with a serrated edge.

Bag length, speed, seal temperature, coding and colour-mark detection are all set from the touch screen, and quenched transmission parts keep the machine stable over long shifts. Plastic, aluminium and metallised films from 0.02 to 0.15 mm run on the same machine.

Frequently Asked Questions

What buyers ask before ordering a brush packing machine.

What speed can it reach?

Up to 90 bags per minute, limited by how consistently the feeder presents brushes.

Which films run on it?

Plastic, aluminium and pure metallised films from 0.02 to 0.15 mm, up to 250 mm wide.

Can it print a date code?

Yes. A coding device is fitted at the seal and set from the touch screen.

Does it handle printed film?

Photoelectric colour-mark tracking keeps printed film registered.

What bag sizes are available?

Bag width 30-110 mm and length up to 220 mm, with product height up to 70 mm. The former is built for the size ordered.
