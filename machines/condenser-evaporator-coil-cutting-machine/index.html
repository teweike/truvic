Condenser / Evaporator Coil Cutting Machine

Cores come off the assembly line as a long block and have to be split to the shape the unit needs. This machine cuts with six blades, half cut or full cut as the process requires, on both sides, with servo feeding so the cut depth stays at 15 mm and the block edge stays square. Coil size is built to the customer's specification, which is what makes it a fit for a specific coil range rather than a compromise.

Key Advantages

Why a dedicated coil saw beats cutting on a general press.

Blades made for fin packs

Six blades cut the fin and tube block without tearing the fins, which a shear or a band saw does at this width.

15 mm depth, repeatable

A fixed cutting depth with servo feeding means the block edge stays square along the full length.

Both sides, one pass

Double-sided cutting squares the block on both faces, so the coil sits straight in the housing.

Built to your coil range

Coil size is customized, so the table, the guides and the gantry span match the coils you actually build.

Half cut when you need it

Half cut leaves the block held together for a later operation, full cut separates it; the same machine does both.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers ask before a coil cutting machine is ordered.

Half cut or full cut, which do I need?

Both are available on the same machine. Half cut is used where the block is still processed afterwards; full cut separates the block for the next station.

What coil sizes can it take?

The machine is built to the customer's coil range. Send the width, length and block thickness and the table and gantry span are matched to it.

Why six blades?

Six blades cut across the fin pack in one stroke without tearing the fins, and the set is used for both half and full depth cuts.

How deep does it cut?

15 mm cutting depth, which covers the coil block thickness in this range; the depth is set for the coil being cut.

Does it need a special foundation?

No. A level concrete floor and levelling pads are enough, plus clearance for the coil length on the infeed and outfeed side.
