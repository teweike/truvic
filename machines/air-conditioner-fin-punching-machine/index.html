HVAC COIL · ALUMINUM FIN STAMPING

Air Conditioner Fin Punching Machine

A complete fin stamping line, not just a press: the aluminium foil is pulled from a single-head active decoiler through an oil-through device, stamped on a 450 kN high-speed press with a precision progressive die, then absorbed and stacked by a movable double-station collector. A phototube loop keeps the foil tension steady at 100 to 120 strokes per minute, and the PLC puller counts holes and sets the stacking quantity, so the line runs unattended between coil changes.

Key Advantages

What keeps fin quality and output steady shift after shift.

Fins come out flat

The oil-through device and the die lubrication keep the strip film even, so fins leave the die flat with clear profiles and no hole sinking.

100–120 strokes, set by the job

Stroke rate is set within this range according to fin height and whether single or double stroke is used, so tall fins are not pushed past their limit.

Coil change without a re-setup

New foil roll, new die height and a new stacking quantity are the only changes between jobs; the loop control takes the new roll without stopping the press.

One operator, two stations

The double-station collector lets the operator empty one stack while the other fills, which is what turns the line into a one-person job.

Die and spares from the same supplier

The progressive die, its spare inserts and the press are quoted together, so wear parts match the press stroke and shut height.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers ask before ordering a fin line.

Is the die included?

Yes. The line is quoted with the progressive die for your fin drawing, and spare inserts are listed separately so you can stock the wear parts you will actually use.

How fast can it run?

100 to 120 strokes per minute. The exact setting depends on fin height and on whether single or double stroke is used, and it is set on the PLC.

What foil does it take?

Aluminium foil with OD 850 mm, ID 75 mm and width up to 330 mm, which covers most coil widths in HVAC work.

Can I run a different fin later?

Yes, by changing the die and the stacking quantity. The press stroke and shut height range accept a second die set, and die height is 210 to 270 mm.

How much floor space is needed?

8 x 2 m for the line itself, plus a roll storage rack beside the decoiler and clearance at the collector end.
