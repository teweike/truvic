Rim Weld Seam Rolling Machine

A C-frame rolling machine that takes the planed seam the last step to flush. The rim is clamped either side of the seam and a flat upper roll is pressed onto a crowned lower roll, rolling the seam level with the parent metal and work-hardening it as it goes — 12 to 15 seconds a rim, on strip 3 to 6 mm thick.

Key Advantages

Why the seam is rolled after it is planed.

Level and work-hardened in one pass

The roll does two things at once: it brings the seam down to the parent metal and it works the material, which raises the hardness at the seam.

The frame holds the force on axis

The C-frame runs on guide rails with four roller bearings per side, so the rolling force cannot push the frame off line and change the rolled size.

Clamp force from a lever

The lever clamps give a high clamping force from two small cylinders, and the setting does not change with strip thickness.

Crowned lower roll

The lower roll crown defines where the seam is worked, which is what gives an even surface along the weld.

One station for planing and rolling

The rolling machine shares its hydraulic and electrical supply with the planing and end-cutting stations, which keeps the line simple.

Set by handwheel

The upper roll height is set by handwheel for the strip thickness, so a rim size change is a setting, not a rebuild.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers ask about seam rolling.

Is rolling different from planing?

Yes. Planing cuts the flash off and leaves 0.2 mm above the parent metal; rolling then presses that allowance down flush and works the material at the same time.

Does the roll setting change with rim size?

The upper roll height is set by handwheel for the strip thickness. The clamp needs no reset, because the lever design follows the material.

Why is the lower roll crowned?

A slight crown gives a defined contact line, so the seam is worked evenly across its width instead of only at the edges.

Can it run without the planing station?

It can, but the seam then has more material to move and the roll works harder. The pair is designed to run together.

What oil does the station need?

A 1200 L tank filled with hydraulic oil to the specification we supply; the oil itself is supplied by the buyer.
