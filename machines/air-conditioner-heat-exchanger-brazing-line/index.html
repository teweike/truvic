Air Conditioner Heat Exchanger Brazing Line

An automatic brazing line for the small-U joints of folded air-conditioner heat exchangers. Folded coils enter on a twin-roller chain conveyor, locate under the torch station, and the torch rows travel along the joint line while the coil stays still. After brazing the fixture releases, the torch head rises, the lift table drops and the conveyor carries the coil out to the next operation.

The line measures the height at both ends of the coil, calculates the slope and drives the torch rows up and down while they travel, so the flame follows the joint curve on top-discharge outdoor coils. Two torch groups each have their own gas flow control, with 30 recipes stored in the 10.4-inch touch screen.

Key Advantages

What the line changes on a coil shop floor.

Flame follows the coil

The line measures both ends, calculates the slope and moves the torch rows while they travel, so a folded or top-discharge coil gets the same joint quality along its length.

Two independent torch groups

Each group has its own gas flow control, so a two-row coil can be brazed at matched flame settings rather than one compromised setting.

Gas safety built in

Ratio limits, flashback arrestors, nitrogen purge on power failure, gas concentration alarm and pressure alarms are part of the line, not add-ons.

Output set by conveyor speed

A 1-5 m/min VFD conveyor and 30 stored recipes let one line run several coil sizes without mechanical changeover.

Fins protected

Air cooling and water mist ahead of the torch keep over-burn to six fins or fewer on hydrophilic foil and eliminate burnt fins on plain foil.

Repeatable joints

Filler lap of 3.5 mm or more and a 3.0 MPa nitrogen leak test give a joint that passes the same check every cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What coil makers ask before ordering a brazing line.

Which coils can the line braze?

Folded air-conditioner heat exchangers with copper tubes of Ф9.52 or Ф15.8 and lengths up to 2,000 mm. Other sizes are quoted with a matched conveyor and torch layout.

How does it handle a sloped joint line?

The line measures the height at both ends of the coil, calculates the curve and drives the torch rows up and down as they travel, so the flame stays on the joint.

What keeps the fins from burning?

Compressed-air cooling and a water mist ahead of the torch, plus stored recipes that set flame size per coil type. The acceptance limit is six over-burnt fins or fewer on hydrophilic foil.

Is the gas system safe?

The line includes ratio limits with auto shut-off, flashback arrestors, nitrogen purge on power failure, a combustible-gas alarm with extinguishing and pressure alarms on every gas.

How many recipes can be stored?

30 parameter sets in the touch screen, each recalled by coil type so changeover is a recipe call rather than a mechanical reset.
