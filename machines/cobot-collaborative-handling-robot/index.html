Cobot - Collaborative / Handling Robot

A welding cobot you program by dragging, not by teaching points through a pendant menu. Grab the end manipulator, move the torch to the joint in six directions, and press a button to record the position, the motion type, the weld instruction and the weave. Speed, welding and weave parameters adjust on the handle, and the display shows speed, pose, command and weld data as you go. Six axes, 5 kg payload and 906 mm reach put it over a fixture or a small weldment without a fence; ±0.02 mm repeatability keeps the torch where you put it.

Key Advantages

Why drag-to-teach changes the economics of a small weldment.

Programmed by moving the torch

The operator pulls the cobot to the joint, presses a button and the position, motion and weld instruction are recorded. No point list, no coordinate entry — a new part is taught in the time it takes to trace its joints.

6 axes, 906 mm reach

Enough reach to cover a fixture or a small weldment from one base position, with ±360° on most joints so the torch can approach from either side.

±0.02 mm, so the second piece matches

High repeatability means the taught path is the path every cycle. The variation in a manual weld — travel speed, angle, weave — is replaced by one recorded motion.

Torch off in one screw

The torch releases with a single screw, so the cobot can be moved into a tight space or re-tooled for a different joint in seconds rather than a service visit.

Magnetic base option

With the optional magnetic base the cobot mounts straight on the workpiece — useful for large fabrications that cannot come to a fixture table.

Frequently Asked Questions

What buyers ask before putting a cobot on a weld cell.

Do we need a programmer to set up a new part?

No. Teaching is done by dragging the cobot to the joint and pressing a button on the handle, with the position, motion type and weld instruction recorded on the spot. A new part is taught by the operator, not an integrator.

What can it actually lift?

5 kg at the flange, and that figure includes the torch and the cable drag. If your torch and cable package is heavy, tell us before the order so the payload is checked against the real load.

How accurate is it compared with manual welding?

Repeatability is ±0.02 mm, so the taught path repeats exactly. That is the difference between a weld that varies with the operator's hand and one that does not.

Can it work on large parts?

Yes, with the optional magnetic base it mounts directly on the workpiece instead of a table — practical for fabrications too large to bring to a fixture.

What environment does it need?

0–45 °C and 20–80% RH without condensation, in an IP54-rated package. Keep the cabinet out of direct dust and water spray.

Is the cart included?

The cart is optional and quoted separately. It makes the cobot movable between stations; the magnetic base is the alternative for mounting on the part itself.
