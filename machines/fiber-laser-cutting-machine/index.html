Key Advantages

Why this configuration holds up in daily sheet cutting.

Kerb 0.08–0.1 mm

A tight kerf is what lets nested parts keep their fit. Auto-focus and 1.0 G acceleration hold it through corners, not only on straight runs.

3D files straight in

The control reads 3D formats and extracts the section and path itself, so a part model goes to the machine without a separate CAM step.

100 m/min traverse

Rapid traverse between contours cuts idle time on a 3-metre sheet, which is where a laser cell usually loses its hours.

1500 × 3000 mm

The standard 5 × 10 ft sheet fits with margin for clamping and for parts that run to the edge.

One 20GP container

The whole cell — bed, gantry, chiller, control — loads in a single 20GP container, which keeps freight and unloading simple.

Serviceable by design

Auto lubrication, replaceable protective window and standard nozzles: the wear items are consumables you can stock.

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions buyers ask before a laser cell is ordered.

What thickness can the machine cut?

Up to 10 mm carbon steel and up to 5 mm stainless steel with the 1500 W source. Thinner sheet cuts faster and with a tighter kerf; the cutting parameters for each material and thickness are set in the control.

Do I need a separate CAM programmer?

Not for most work. The control reads 3D formats such as IGS and extracts the section and cutting path itself, and parts are nested inside the control.

What gas supply is needed?

O2 at 6–8 bar for carbon steel, N2 at 15–20 bar for stainless, and compressed air at 15–30 bar. Bottles, a tank or an on-site generator all work if the pressure and flow are there.

How is it shipped?

The complete cell loads in one 20GP container, packed in three layers with air-cushion film. A forklift is needed at both ends.

How long does installation take?

10–15 working days on site, including assembly, alignment, test cutting and operator training.

What does the warranty cover?

2.5 years on the machine and 12 months on the laser source, cutting head and chiller. Lenses, protective windows and nozzles are consumables and are quoted as spares.

Can the table size be changed?

The 1500 × 3000 mm table is the standard build. Other table sizes are quoted on request with the same head, control and chiller package.
