Automatic Flame Brazing Machine for Copper Manifolds

A rotary brazing cell for copper manifolds, built around a two-station table: the operator loads the next manifold at the manual station while the other station brazes automatically. Two flame torches sit on separate servo drives, so they can braze two short branch sections at once or travel together along one long seam up to 500 mm. The phosphorous copper wire is fed by servo at a preset delay, speed and quantity, and the PLC stores gas flow, preheat, heating and cycle times as recipes, which is what makes the second, third and hundredth manifold come out the same.

Key Advantages

What a turntable and two servo torches change on a manifold line.

Load and braze at the same time

Assembly happens at one station while the other brazes, so the operator is not waiting for the flame to finish.

Two torches, one or two seams

Short manifolds are brazed by both torches at once; a long manifold is brazed by both torches travelling together.

Recipes, not operator memory

Heating, preheat, wire delay, feed speed and quantity, and cycle time are stored, so a change of shift does not change the joint.

Gas ratio held automatically

Oxygen and fuel are controlled by the intelligent gas circuit, which keeps temperature stable and alarms on low pressure.

A table that stays flat

The stainless steel table is water cooled and damped, so it does not distort under brazing heat and the fixture stays true.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers ask before a manifold brazing cell is specified.

How many manifolds per hour?

Throughput depends on the branch count and the brazing cycle, which is set per manifold family. The two-station table means loading overlaps brazing, so the cycle is limited by the longer of the two, not their sum. Send the drawing and the required output and the cycle will be quoted against it.

Can it braze one long seam instead of two short ones?

Yes. The two torches have separate servo drives and can travel together along one long seam, or work independently on two sections of a manifold.

What wire does it use?

Phosphorous copper brazing wire of 1.2 to 1.6 mm, on spools of up to 10 kg, fed automatically at a preset delay, speed and quantity.

Is the gas mixture fixed?

The intelligent gas circuit controls oxygen and fuel ratio and holds the pressure and flow that were preset. If the supply pressure falls, the machine alarms with sound and light instead of brazing a cold joint.

How is the fixture aligned?

The table indexes within ±15′ and repeats within ±30′, and fixture mounting is held to ±0.30 mm horizontal and ±0.20 mm elevation, so the joint sits under the torch the same way every cycle.
