Toothbrush Tufting & Trimming Machine

A combined tufting and trimming machine for toothbrush heads. The head is clamped in one of four self-locking fixtures, bristles are implanted at the programmed hole pattern, and the same cycle trims the tufted bristles to a consistent length or profile with 5-8 grinding discs.

X and Y strokes of 50 mm and 20 mm cover the full head area, and the U axis handles colour change between tufting positions. The 10-inch touch screen stores brush programs in a dynamic library, switches the interface to English and raises alarms for faults or low bristle supply.

Key Advantages

What the machine changes for a brush line.

Tuft and trim in one cycle

Implanting and trimming run on the same fixture, so the brush is not unclamped between operations.

Wide hole range

1.0-3.2 mm holes and 50 / 20 mm strokes cover adult, child and interdental head layouts.

Colour change on the U axis

Multi-colour brushes are programmed per tufting position instead of changing the machine setup.

Programs, not setups

Thousands of brush programs are stored and recalled from the 10-inch screen; changeover is a program call.

Guarded trimming

Trimming runs behind a guarded door with a viewing window, keeping dust and discs away from the operator.

Serviceable fixtures

Four self-locking clamps strip automatically, so worn fixtures are changed without realigning the head.

Frequently Asked Questions

What brush makers ask before ordering.

Which brush heads fit?

Heads with 8-14 tufts, 1.0-3.2 mm holes and a layout inside the 50 / 20 mm X-Y stroke. Send the drawing and we confirm the needle plate.

Can it trim to a curved profile?

Yes. The Z swing angle and the grinding fixture set the bristle length or curvature, and the profile is stored with the brush program.

How is colour change handled?

The U axis indexes between colour positions, so a multi-colour brush runs without stopping to change bristle boxes.

How many programs can be stored?

A dynamic library holding thousands of brush programs, recalled from the touch screen.

Is the interface in English?

Yes. The HMI ships with an English interface, switchable at the screen.
