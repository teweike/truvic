HVAC COIL · TUBE EXPANSION

Vertical Hydraulic Copper Tube Expander

One cycle does three jobs: the tubes are expanded into the fin stack, the tube ends are enlarged, and the ends are flared. That is what makes the copper tube and the aluminium fins join tightly, and tight joints are what the heat transfer of the coil depends on. Expansion force comes from a 14 MPa hydraulic system with six 12 kW pump motors, the stroke is set per coil height, and the head is guided vertically so a tall core expands evenly from bottom to top.

Key Advantages

Why the expansion step decides the coil's heat transfer.

Flare and expansion together

Expanding, end forming and flaring in one cycle removes a second handling step and a second chance of damaging the tube end.

Even force over a tall core

Six pump motors and a guided vertical head keep pressure constant from the bottom row to the top row, so the coil expands evenly.

30 seconds, then change the coil

Low-speed expansion pass, fast approach and a 10 m/min return add up to 30 seconds per cycle, before the workpiece change.

Hydraulic system built for a shift

300 L tank and 14 MPa working pressure mean the oil has the volume to stay cool and the pressure to stay steady.

Accessories that match the coil

Expander assembly, intermediate base plate, stripper plate, feeding receiver and flaring parts are supplied as a matched set for your core.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers ask before an expander is ordered.

Can it expand and flare at the same time?

Yes. Expansion, end enlargement and flaring happen in one cycle, with separate flaring parts for the second and third flare so the joint profile matches your brazing fixture.

What core size can it take?

Tube OD, rows, tubes per row, pitches and the maximum core size are all built to your drawing, and the stroke is set per coil height.

Why six pump motors?

Force has to stay constant over the full height of a tall core. Six 12 kW motors keep the expansion pass at pressure without slowing down, which is what keeps the cycle at 30 seconds.

Does it handle thin-wall tube?

Wall thickness is a specification item. Send the OD and wall and the expander assembly is sized so the tube does not split at the joint.

What maintenance does it need?

Oil level and temperature checks per shift, filter change on schedule, and a check of the expander head and flaring parts for wear at each tooling change.
