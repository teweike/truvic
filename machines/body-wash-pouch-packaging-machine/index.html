Body Wash Pouch Packaging Machine

An automatic pouch packing machine for body wash, shower gel and liquid detergent. Pre-made bags are picked from the magazine, opened on the rotary stations, filled from the hopper by weight, sealed and discharged — 10 to 50 bags per minute depending on product and fill.

Four-side seal, three-side seal, shaped, paper and other pre-made bags run on the same machine. The PLC and touch screen hold the recipe, show faults on screen and alarm on heater failure or abnormal running, while colour-mark tracking keeps the seal position on printed bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

What buyers ask before ordering a pouch machine.

Which bag styles run on it?

Four-side seal, three-side seal, shaped, paper and other pre-made bags within 80-160 mm × 110-230 mm.

What fill weights are possible?

10-500 g, depending on product density and the dosing set fitted.

Is it suitable for viscous products?

Yes, body wash and shower gel are typical products; the dosing set is matched to viscosity at order.

How are seal positions kept on printed bags?

Photoelectric colour-mark tracking with a digitally entered seal position.

What happens on a fault?

The PLC detects the fault, stops the cycle and shows the cause on the touch screen.
