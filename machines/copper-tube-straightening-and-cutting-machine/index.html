Key Advantages

What the line buys the coil shop, station by station.

One frame, no handling between steps

Pay-off, straightening, cutting and bending sit on one base. Tube is never re-loaded between stations, so a coil goes in and hairpins come out.

Ends that line up in the jig

Centre distance between the two ends is held to ±0.1 mm and the two ends to within 1.0 mm, which is what the brazing fixture and the coil assembly stack need.

Chipless cutting, open ends

The tube is parted without a saw, so there is no burr and no chip to blow out, and the bore at the cut end stays above the minimum the expander needs.

Three tubes per cycle

Up to three tubes are straightened, cut and bent together, which is how a hairpin line keeps up with a coil press.

No wrinkle at the loop

Flattening stays under 15% and wall thinning under 30% at the bend, so the hairpin holds its section through expansion.

Tooling for two tube sizes

The line is set for Φ7 mm and Φ9.52 mm; change-over is a tooling and recipe change, not a rebuild.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions coil shops ask before a hairpin line is ordered.

Does the line cut and bend, or only cut?

Both. The machine decoils, straightens, chipless-cuts to length and bends the tube into a long U in the same cycle, then unloads the hairpin. It is a hairpin line, not only a straightener and cutter.

Which tube sizes can run on one machine?

The line is built for Φ7 mm and Φ9.52 mm copper tube, each with its own bending radius and tooling set. Other diameters are quoted as a separate build.

How many hairpins per minute?

Cycle time is under 12 s for hairpins up to 800 mm and under 21 s up to 2100 mm, with up to three tubes formed in the same cycle. Actual output depends on the hairpin length mix.

Why does the cut end bore matter?

The cut end is where the expander or the brazing sleeve enters. The chipless cut keeps the bore above Φ5.6 mm on Φ7 tube and above Φ8 mm on Φ9.52 tube, so insertion and expansion stay reliable.

How is the hairpin measured?

Length is servo-fed and closed-loop measured. Centre distance between the two ends is held within ±0.1 mm measured 14 mm away from the loop, and the two ends to within 1.0 mm of each other.

What does the line need in the plant?

Three-phase 380 V supply and clean, dry air at 0.4–0.6 MPa, plus a level floor. One operator runs the line; coil and tooling changes are manual.

What is included with the machine?

The complete line on one base, the tooling set for the ordered tube diameter, the PLC recipe list, a spare set of cutter and mandrel wear parts, the English manual, and installation with operator training on site.
