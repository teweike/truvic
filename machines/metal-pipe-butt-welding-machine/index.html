WELDING · PIPE BUTT WELDING

Metal Pipe Butt Welding Machine

A butt welding machine for joining the ends of straight round steel pipe of 6 to 8 mm wall. The frame and the C-shaped clamps are welded carbon steel, stress relieved and precision machined, roughly 3000 x 2000 x 1000 mm and 3.3 t, so the upsetting force goes into the joint rather than into frame deflection. Current comes from a water-cooled, high duty cycle transformer with secondary copper connections, which is what allows the machine to run pipe after pipe without cooling down. A mechanical shield blocks spatter at the arc, and a steel blade scrapes the flash off the joint after the weld.

Key Advantages

Why the frame and the transformer matter more than the control panel.

Rigidity before features

A welded, stress-relieved, machined frame and C-clamps hold alignment at the moment of upset, which is when a light frame shows up as a bent joint.

Continuous duty

A water-cooled transformer with a high duty cycle means pipe after pipe, without waiting for the machine to cool.

Spatter stays at the joint

The mechanical shield sits between the arc and the operator and is switched manually, so it is either on or off, not partly on.

Flash removed at the machine

The steel scraper blade takes the flash off the joint before the pipe leaves the clamps, so the next step is not cleaning.

Interface in your language

Chinese and English are standard on the touch screen, and another language can be configured for the operators who will run it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers ask before a pipe butt welder is ordered.

What wall thickness can it weld?

6 to 8 mm on straight round steel pipe, with the clamping and current settings matched to that wall.

Can it weld stainless or aluminium pipe?

The machine as specified is built for carbon steel round pipe. Other materials are reviewed against the required joint and, if suitable, quoted as a separate configuration.

What is the shortest pipe it accepts?

300 mm. Longer pipe is supported by a steel frame, which is built to the length you run.

How far can the power cabinet be?

Within 100 m, with a cable of at least 36 mm² between cabinet and machine, so the welding current is not lost in the cable.

Is the flash removed automatically?

The joint is welded with a mechanical shield in place, then the steel scraper blade removes the flash at the machine before the pipe is released.
