Semi-Automatic U-Hairpin Copper Tube Bending Machine

This machine bends straight cut copper tube into U hairpins for air conditioner and heat exchanger coils. Only loading the blank tube and unloading the finished hairpin are manual; clamping, bending and ejecting are automatic, which is why the cycle stays inside 5 seconds on short tubes. Length control is closed loop, so both ends of a hairpin match and the bend area keeps its wall thickness through expansion later on the coil line.

Key Advantages

What the semi-automatic layout buys you on short hairpins.

Manual load, automatic bend

The operator only loads a cut tube and lifts the finished hairpin. Everything that decides quality, clamping, bending and ejection, is done by the machine.

Five seconds, not five minutes

Under 5 seconds per hairpin up to 400 mm, so one operator keeps a coil line supplied without a second machine.

Ends that match

Both ends within 1.0 mm and centres within ±0.1 mm, which is the difference between a hairpin that drops in and one that has to be pushed.

Bend area ready for expansion

Flattening under 10% and thinning under 15% leave enough wall for the expander to work without splitting the bend.

Tooling for each tube size

Bend die and clamps are changed per diameter and radius, so one machine covers the range of coils you build.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers ask before ordering a hairpin bender.

Is it fully automatic?

No, and that is deliberate. Loading the blank tube and taking off the finished hairpin are manual; clamping, bending and ejection are automatic. That keeps the price down without giving up bend accuracy.

How long a hairpin can it bend?

Length is set by the tooling and by your coil, and the cycle is under 3 seconds up to 200 mm and under 5 seconds up to 400 mm.

Does it work with the return bender?

They are different jobs. This machine makes long U hairpins from straight tube; the return bender makes small U and crossover bends from cut tube for the coil ends. Both usually sit in the same shop.

What about wall thinning?

Thinning at the bend stays under 15% and flattening under 10%, which is what allows the tube to be expanded afterwards without cracking at the bend.

Can one machine cover several tube sizes?

Yes, by changing the bend die and clamps. Send the diameter and radius list and the tooling sets are quoted with the machine.
