RIM MAKING · EXPANDING

Vertical Rim Expanding Press

A vertical press that expands a welded and rolled rim to its finished diameter. A 2,000 kN cylinder in the crown drives the expanding die down through the rim, the slide stroke and the expansion are set by proximity switches, and the station carries a flattening and valve hole punching unit so the rim leaves with its flat face and valve hole done.

Key Advantages

Why a vertical expanding press on a rim line.

Diameter held by a switch position

The expanding stroke is set by proximity switches, so the finished diameter is a repeatable machine setting rather than an operator judgement.

Three operations at one station

Expanding, flattening and valve hole punching are carried on the same press, so the rim is not handled again between them.

Force where it is needed

With the cylinder in the crown the expanding force runs straight down the frame, which is why a welded body of this size can carry 2,000 kN.

Protected against its own force

Over-stroke and over-pressure protection plus an emergency stop cover the two failure modes that matter on a press working to a switch position.

Fast approach, slow work

100 mm/s approach and 5–20 mm/s working speed give a short cycle without working the material at impact speed.

Fits the line

Sized to take the rim straight from the seam rolling station, with the blank support rack as the loading point.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers ask about rim expanding.

How is the finished diameter set?

By the expanding stroke, which is set with proximity switches on the die. The same switch position repeats the same diameter.

Does it also punch the valve hole?

Yes. The press carries a flattening and valve hole punching mechanism, so the flat face and the valve hole are done at the same station.

Why is the frame vertical?

Because the expanding force then runs down the frame in a straight line, which is the stiffest arrangement for a press of this size.

How big a rim can it take?

Up to 1,300 mm daylight and 800 mm slide stroke. Send the rim range and we confirm the die and the switch positions.

Is it guarded?

Yes: guards on the working zone, over-stroke and over-pressure protection, emergency stop and filter clogging alarm.
