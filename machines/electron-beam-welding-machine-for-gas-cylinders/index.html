Electron Beam Welding Machine for Gas Cylinders

An 80 kV, 8 kW electron beam welding machine built for gas cylinder and pressure vessel work. The gun delivers at least 30 mm penetration in stainless steel and aluminium, at a depth-to-width ratio of 15:1 or better, so the cylinder's longitudinal and circumferential seams close in a single pass without groove preparation.

The 1,700 × 1,400 × 1,600 mm chamber takes a workpiece up to 600 × 800 × 700 mm on the faceplate, with X-Y-R axes under CNC linkage. The gun has its own turbomolecular pumping set and stays under vacuum while the chamber door is opened, so each cycle only pulls the chamber down — six minutes or less.

Key Advantages

What the machine changes for a cylinder shop.

One-pass cylinder seams

30 mm penetration at 15:1 depth-to-width closes longitudinal and circumferential seams without a V-groove.

Narrow heat-affected zone

Vacuum welding keeps the seam zone narrow, so cylinder distortion stays low and post-weld straightening is minimal.

Gun stays under vacuum

The gun chamber holds vacuum while the door is open, so only the chamber is pumped each cycle — six minutes or less.

X-Y-R linkage

Three axes run under CNC, covering vertical and horizontal circumferential seams, straight seams and curved seams on the same machine.

Radiation and X-ray control

Carbon-steel chamber wall of 30 mm or more holds leakage at or below 2.5 µSv/h at the wall.

Aluminium-capable

A metal-vapour protection device suppresses discharge when welding aluminium alloy cylinders.

Frequently Asked Questions

What cylinder makers ask before ordering.

What wall thickness can be welded?

At least 30 mm of stainless steel or aluminium in a single pass at 8 kW. Thicker sections can be welded from both sides with a matched seam design.

Does the cylinder need groove preparation?

No V-groove is required. The beam micro-fits the joint faces, so square-butt or close-fit seams are welded as machined.

How long is a cycle?

Chamber pump-down takes six minutes or less; the weld itself is one rotation or one linear pass depending on the seam. Total cycle is confirmed against your cylinder size.

Is the machine safe for operators?

The chamber wall holds X-ray leakage at or below 2.5 µSv/h, and the vacuum sequence is interlocked so the beam cannot start with the door open.

Can it weld aluminium cylinders?

Yes. The gun carries a metal-vapour protection device that suppresses discharge during aluminium welding.
