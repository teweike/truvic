Machine in detail

Control station, bending head and the bends the machine produces.

Why this machine

Why a dedicated small-U machine beats bending on a general bender.

30 to 40 pieces per minute

The cycle is built for one bend form. Feed, bend and cut run together, so output is counted in pieces per minute rather than minutes per piece.

U height within ±0.5 mm

U height, radius and the distance between the two ends are set in the recipe and held by the servo, which is what automatic brazing and stacking need.

Three tubes in one cycle

Up to three 12.7 mm tubes are straightened, bent and cut in the same cycle, so one machine covers a full coil station.

Guard hood over the bend

The bending head runs behind a hood and the machine carries a stack light, so the operator loads coils and takes bends without standing in the bend path.

Clean surface, tight burr

No wrinkle, scratch, crack, dimple or bulge at the bend and burr under 0.2 mm at the ends: the part goes straight into brazing without a dressing step.

Tooling for the bend, not a general bender

The head is built around the small U. Changing to another bend is a tooling and recipe change, not a new setup on a tube bender.

Questions buyers ask

What coil shops ask before ordering a small-U bender.

Can it make both U bends and C bends?

Yes. The same head makes small U bends and crossover C bends; the difference is the tooling set and the recipe, both quoted with the machine for the drawings you send.

What is the real output?

30 to 40 pieces per minute with up to three 12.7 mm tubes in the cycle. Output holds as long as the bend form stays the same; changing forms stops the line for a tooling change.

What tube can it run?

1/2 inch (12.7 mm) copper tube from a 180 kg reel, OD up to Φ1100 mm and core ID Φ130 mm. Other diameters are quoted as a separate build.

How tight can the bend be?

Minimum bending radius is set by the drawing, and the mandrel is built to it. Send the U height, radius and leg lengths and we confirm the mandrel and the flattening you can expect.

Does the machine cut the tube too?

Yes. Tube is cut to length after bending, with burr under 0.2 mm at both ends, so the bend goes into brazing without a dressing step.

What does it need in the plant?

10 kW at three-phase 380 V and clean, dry air at 0.4–0.6 MPa, on a level floor. One operator runs the machine.

How is it shipped?

The machine travels with the bending head guarded and the tooling boxed separately. Mandrels and the spare kit are packed with the machine, and the manual goes in the crate.
