Automatic Rim Blank Loading Rack

A gantry loading rack for a rim line. Vacuum suction cups lift a flat steel blank from the stack, the carriage carries it across the frame on guide rails, and a driven roller table hands it to the ring-forming station — one blank per cycle, 18 seconds or faster, transfer speed adjustable, controlled together with the forming machine.

Key Advantages

Why a dedicated loading rack pays for itself on a rim line.

One blank per cycle, no manual lift

The operator sets the stack, not the pace. Cups pick and the carriage transfers, so the forming machine is fed at a steady beat instead of waiting for a hand lift.

Every moving part guided and adjustable

Guides and fine adjustment on the carriage and the pick head mean the blank lands square in the forming pass, which is what keeps the ring round.

Pick confirmed before the move

A proximity switch on the cups confirms the blank before the carriage travels, so an empty or slipped pick is caught at the stack, not at the forming rolls.

One control with the forming machine

The loader runs interlocked with the roll-marking and ring-forming machine: the two stations work as one unit rather than two machines with an operator between them.

Independent air supply

The vacuum and the cylinders have their own air feed, so the loader keeps its cycle even when another station on the line draws air.

Built for service

Control elements sit on the frame next to the actuators they drive, with short piping and accessible guards, which keeps maintenance time down.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers ask before a loading rack is added to a rim line.

Does the rack replace the operator?

It replaces the manual lift at the entry end: the operator sets the stack and supervises the line, the rack picks and transfers each blank.

How fast is a cycle?

18 seconds or more per piece, speed adjustable. The real limit is usually the forming machine, not the rack.

Can it feed an existing forming machine?

Yes, provided the machine has a roller infeed the rack can hand over to. The pick position and the handover height are set on site.

What happens if a blank slips off the cups?

A proximity switch on the cups checks the pick before the carriage moves, so the cycle stops at the stack rather than sending an empty transfer.

Does it need its own compressor?

No, but it needs 0.6 MPa and 100 L/min or more. An independent air feed is recommended so other stations cannot starve the loader.
