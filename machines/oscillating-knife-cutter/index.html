Oscillating Knife Cutter

A digital cutting table that works with a knife instead of a die: the oscillating blade moves vertically at high frequency while the gantry carries it over a vacuum-held sheet, so patterns change by loading a file rather than by making a tool. The frame is welded from 6 mm steel tube and the cutting surface is a 7-series aluminium honeycomb panel, both chosen so the table neither vibrates under the gantry nor deforms over a long shift. Vacuum is split into zones that are switched individually, so a small part does not need the open table masked, and one or more heads can carry oscillating, circular, V-cut, milling, drag or pneumatic tools for the material in front of them.

Key Advantages

What an oscillating knife changes against a die press or a hand knife.

Minutes per pattern, not weeks per die

A new pattern is a new file. Three 4D or 5D car mat sets can be cut inside three minutes, so a custom order does not wait for tooling.

At least 5 percent material saved

Patterns are nested and checked before the knife starts, which avoids the hidden loss that appears when a worker cuts by eye.

No stock of finished patterns

Cut what is ordered, when it is ordered, so the storage room does not have to hold a size range in advance.

Several parts in one pass

Multiple heads cut identical parts at once, and zoned vacuum holds small pieces without masking the rest of the table.

Safety built around the gantry

Infrared sensors, anti-collision response and four emergency stops, because the operator works close to a moving head.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers ask before a knife cutting table is ordered.

How thick can it cut?

Up to 60 mm, but the practical limit depends on the material: foam and EPE go near the maximum, dense rubber and thick sponge leather need a slower feed and the right blade.

Can it cut several parts at once?

Yes. The machine can carry one head or several heads, and identical parts are cut in one pass. Tools are changed on the same head for different cutting needs.

What materials does it handle?

Fabric and garment flexible materials, sponge leather, PVC, soft glass, silicone, rubber and cardboard, plus EVA, acrylic and corrugated board in sample and small-batch work.

Do I need a die or a mould?

No. Cutting is mechanical with an oscillating knife, so a new pattern is a new file. That is the point of the machine for custom and short-run production.

What accuracy should I plan on?

Repeat accuracy within 0.1 mm. What the part actually gets also depends on how well the material is held, which is why the vacuum is split into zones.
