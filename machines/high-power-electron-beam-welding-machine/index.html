High-Power Electron Beam Welding Machine

A 15 kW, 80 kV electron beam welding machine for thick-section work. The gun welds 50 mm of stainless steel in a single pass at a depth-to-width ratio above 17:1, so heavy wall parts are closed without multi-pass welding or a large heat-affected zone.

The 1,700 × 1,400 × 1,600 mm chamber has a stainless-steel anti-vapour liner and an electrically driven sliding door, with an X-Y table of 800 × 600 mm carrying more than 1,500 kg and a Ф600 rotary table with tailstock. Four axes run simultaneously under CNC with G-code programming, which covers welds of varying height, thickness and scan angle.

Key Advantages

Why this 15 kW configuration is chosen over a smaller EBW.

50 mm in one pass

A 15 kW beam welds 50 mm of stainless steel without multi-pass sequences, at a depth-to-width ratio above 17:1.

Low distortion on thick parts

The narrow heat-affected zone keeps heavy-section parts straight, cutting post-weld machining.

Large table, heavy load

The 800 × 600 mm table carries more than 1,500 kg, so fixture plates and large housings sit directly on the T-slots.

Four axes at once

X, Y, R and scan run together under G-code, covering welds that change height, thickness and angle along the seam.

Fast, independent pumping

The gun holds vacuum between cycles; the chamber pulls down in ten minutes or less with the door interlocked.

Radiation held at the wall

The stainless-lined 30 mm chamber keeps leakage at or below 1 µSv/h at the wall.

Frequently Asked Questions

What buyers ask before committing to a 15 kW EBW.

How deep can it weld?

50 mm of stainless steel in a single pass; the depth-to-width ratio is above 17:1, so the seam stays narrow relative to its depth.

Can it weld along a curved seam?

Yes. Four axes run simultaneously under G-code, so the beam follows seams that change height, thickness and scan angle.

What size parts fit?

The chamber is 1,700 × 1,400 × 1,600 mm, the table is 800 × 600 mm with more than 1,500 kg capacity, and the rotary table takes more than 400 kg.

Why two chillers?

One unit cools the electron gun and the other cools the vacuum plant, so gun temperature stays stable during long welding cycles.

How is X-ray safety handled?

The 30 mm or thicker chamber wall with a stainless liner holds leakage at or below 1 µSv/h, and the door switch prevents welding with the door open.
