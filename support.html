Support, Shipping & Documents

What happens after the order is signed: warranty and spare parts, packing and shipping, and the export documents that travel with your container.

Warranty & spare parts

Coverage: 12 months from commissioning or 13 months from shipment, whichever comes first, on core mechanical and electrical components. Wear parts, consumables and damage caused by incorrect operation or unauthorised modification are excluded.

Claim process: send the machine serial number, a photo or short video of the fault, and the proforma invoice number. We confirm whether it is a warranty case within two business days and ship the replacement part by courier, usually within 3–7 days for stock items.

Spare parts: a recommended spare-parts list is issued with every order, split into "keep on the shelf" wear items and "order when needed" components. Consumable tooling, molds and belts can be reordered at any time with the serial number.

Technical support: remote guidance by video call during business hours (Mon–Fri, 09:00–18:00 GMT+8). On-site commissioning can be arranged for production lines and large equipment.

Shipping & packing

  • Packing: plywood export cases with fumigation-free treatment, moisture barrier film and bolted skids; small machines are packed in reinforced cartons on pallets.
  • Loading: we send a container loading plan and photos of the packed units before the container is sealed.
  • Terms: EXW, FOB Shanghai or Ningbo, CIF to your port, and door-to-door by sea or rail where the route allows.
  • Timing: booking is made against the delivery date in the proforma invoice; the bill of lading is sent as a scan within two business days of departure, originals by courier if required.
  • Insurance: cargo insurance can be included in a CIF quote on request.

Export documents

Every shipment leaves with a document set matched to your destination and payment method:

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of lading (original, telex release or sea waybill as agreed)
  • Certificate of origin (CCPIT / FORM E / FORM A where applicable)
  • CE declaration or test report for machines where the destination requires it
  • Insurance certificate when quoted CIF
  • Operation manual, wiring diagram and spare-parts list inside the case

If your customs broker needs a specific wording, HS code confirmation or legalisation, tell us before the container is booked — documents are easier to adjust before shipment than after.
