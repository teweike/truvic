Thank You — Your RFQ Is In

A real engineer reads every inquiry. You will get a model recommendation and a factory-direct price within one business day, Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 GMT+8.

What happens next

01 · Technical check. We match your material, thickness or tube size and output per shift to a specific model — not a catalogue page. If something is missing, we ask one short question instead of guessing.

02 · Quote. You receive the price, scope of supply, lead time and packing details in one reply, so you can compare it against other suppliers directly.

03 · Export follow-through. After the order: plywood-case packing, container loading plan, commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin — plus a spare-parts list.
