What to include in your first message

Five lines are usually enough for us to quote accurately:

  • Machine you are looking for, or the job it has to do
  • Material and size — sheet thickness, tube diameter, busbar size or product dimensions
  • Output required per hour or per shift
  • Destination port and voltage / frequency (for example 380V 50Hz or 480V 60Hz)
  • Purchase timeline — immediately, 1–3 months or budgeting stage
