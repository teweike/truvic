Terms of Sale

The commercial terms that apply to quotations and orders, unless a signed contract or proforma invoice states otherwise.

Last updated: November 2026

Quotations

Quotations are valid for 30 days unless another period is stated. Prices are factory-direct, exclusive of import duties and taxes in the destination country, and based on the specification written in the quotation.

Orders and payment

  • An order is confirmed when we receive the signed proforma invoice and the deposit.
  • Standard terms: 30% T/T deposit, 70% before shipment; irrevocable L/C at sight for larger orders.
  • Payment is made only to the bank account named on the proforma invoice. Notify us immediately if any other account is presented.

Production and delivery

Lead time runs from deposit receipt and from the date all technical details — voltage, mould size, product dimensions, colour and labelling — are confirmed in writing. Delivery terms follow Incoterms 2020 as stated in the invoice (EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP).

Inspection

You may inspect the machine before shipment, in person or through a third-party inspection company at your cost. Where inspection is not possible, we send a running video and photo set of the actual machine before packing.

Warranty

12 months from commissioning or 13 months from shipment, whichever comes first, covering core mechanical and electrical components. Wear parts, consumables, and damage from incorrect operation, unauthorised modification or unsuitable power supply are excluded. Warranty covers replacement parts; labour and travel are quoted separately.

Claims and liability

Claims must be reported with the serial number, photos or video and the invoice number. Our liability is limited to repair or replacement of the defective part; we are not liable for loss of production or consequential damages.

Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, port closures, strikes, epidemics and changes in export regulation.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China. Disputes are first handled by negotiation; failing that, by arbitration in Shanghai.
